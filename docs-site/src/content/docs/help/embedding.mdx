---
title: Embedding
description: Embedding a component's story in another page.
---

Every story in the production Storybook can be embedded on its own, without the Storybook interface:

```html
<iframe
  src="https://lemonade-design-system.vercel.app/iframe.html?id=components-<name>--<story>&viewMode=story"
  title="Component name: story name"
></iframe>
```

The list of every story ID is at https://lemonade-design-system.vercel.app/index.json. The component pages on this site embed their stories this way.

Storybook answers 200 even for a story that does not exist, so look at what rendered.
